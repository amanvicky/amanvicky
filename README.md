### 🧠 [Genesis — Business Decoding Engine](https://github.com/amanvicky/Genesis-Business-Decoding-Engine)
Feed it a company (Stripe, Razorpay, your competitor) or a rough idea → get a **14-tab GO/NO-GO dossier**: model viability, compliance traps per country, real revenue from SEC filings, editable pricing math, a red-team panel that argues with your plan. **No API keys, no accounts, nothing leaves your machine.**
13 agents · FastAPI + Next.js · CI · MIT

### 💼 [Leyp — The Job Hunter](https://github.com/amanvicky/leyp)
Self-hosted job-hunt copilot with one rule: **the human approves every send.** Paste a posting → match report → tailored resume + cover letter → nightly agent scan → Gmail inbox with classified replies.
Next.js 16 · React 19 · Prisma 7 · strict TypeScript · Vitest + Playwright · OpenAI/Anthropic/Mistral/Ollama

### 👁 [OpenBroadcast](https://github.com/amanvicky/Open-Broadcast) · [Gazester](https://github.com/amanvicky/Gazester)
Real-time **eye gaze correction** so you look at the camera on video calls — even while reading notes. OpenBroadcast runs on **CPU at 30fps** (no GPU), with a virtual camera for Zoom/Teams/OBS, iris segmentation and a calibration wizard.
**Try it right now, no install:** [openbroadcast-web](https://amanvicky.github.io/openbroadcast-web/) — the browser demo.

### 🕹 [GOD MODE](https://github.com/amanvicky/Skill-God-Mode)
**An AI software company in a single Claude skill.** PM → dev squads → red-team waves → CEO scoring — and nothing ships without the DIAMOND FINISH pass: *"Proof or it didn't happen."* First production run: complete, tested game shipped in ~23 minutes.

### 🛠 [Human-Typer](https://github.com/amanvicky/Human-Typer)
Windows (AutoHotkey v2) typing simulator with human-like timing — variable speed, punctuation pauses, typos that get corrected. For demos, tutorials and remote-desktop sessions.
